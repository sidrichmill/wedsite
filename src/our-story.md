# Our Story

<div class="timeline">

## 1996
    Devon and Sid were born 100 miles apart. Devon in Gap, Pennsylvania and Sid in Lewes, Delaware.

## 2000

    ![Young Devon at the beach](/images/Willard_0075-sq.jpg) ![Young Sid at the beach](/images/2000-sidbeach-sq.jpg)
    Devon vacationed at the Delaware beaches in her childhood. We will never know if we crossed paths earlier in life!

## 2016

    Devon and Sid met through mutual friends while in college, but really got to know each other better in 2016 when we were both living in Philadelphia over the summer.

## 2017

    ![An early photo of Devon and Sid from when they started dating](/images/2017.jpeg)
    We started dating after having an Ergonomics class together where we partnered up!

## 2018

    ![A photo of Sid and Devon graduating college](/images/2018-graduation.jpeg)
    We graduated college, Devon got a job in California and we drove across the country to move all of her worldly belongings. Sid flew back to the East Coast to hunt for jobs while living at his parents house. We didn't know if we would end up living in the save timezone, let alone city, but Fate had plans for us. Sid found a job in Alameda, CA only a few miles from Devon and moved across the country before the end of summer.

## 2019

    ![A photo of Sid Devon and Cairo](/images/2020-cairo.jpeg)
    After a brief stint of fostering, we adopted our beloved boy, Cairo, from a local animal shelter.

## 2020

    ![A photo of Sid Devon during Covid](/images/2020-covid.jpeg)
    We hunkered down to weather the pandemic, doing our best to keep in contact with our families and continue to build community in California.

## 2022

    ![Sid, Cairo, and Devon hugging a giant redwood tree.](/images/2022-roadtrip.jpeg)
    Devon accepted a job offer from Nike and we once again packed our life to move states, this time to Portland, Oregon.

## 2024
    ![Sid, Devon, and Cairo on Devon's birthday](/images/2026-devdaygroup.jpeg)
    Devon and Sid put down roots in Portland, building community and making friends.

## 2026

    ![A photo of Sid Devon on a hill](/images/2026-proposal.jpeg)
    During a wonderful trip to England, Sid proposed to Devon on a hill top while watching the sunset.

</div>
