# Q&A

## Are kids welcome?
We are only inviting children in our immedate family! We have limited space and want the adults to enjoy the evening! Thank you for understanding.

## Are plus ones invited?
We have very limited seats, so we have sent specific invites to all guests. Thank you for understanding!

## What time should I arrive?
The ceremony begins promptly at 5PM — guests should plan to arrive 20–30 minutes beforehand.

## Is there parking on site?
There is street parking around the venue, but ridesharing is a good idea!

## Can I take photos during the ceremony?
We request you put your phones away during the ceramony, there will be profesional photographer and we want to see you in photos, not your phones! 

## Who should I contact with other questions?
Reach out to Devon and Sid directly with any questions!
